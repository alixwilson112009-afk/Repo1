Questions

The things people ask on the call.

Cost, contracts, whether you have to change the software you already run, and whether any of this is legal. Answered here so you don't have to book a call to find out.

What does it cost?

It depends on how much of the path you want covered. Someone who only wants calls answered pays less than someone who wants quotes, reminders, and reviews handled too. There's no long contract either way, so you're not buying a year to find out whether it works.

We go through the number on the call, once we know what you actually need.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. It's month to month, and you can cancel any time.

I already use Jobber, ServiceTitan, or Housecall Pro. Do I have to switch?

Not necessarily. Some businesses keep the software they already use for scheduling and dispatch, and Hero handles the lead side by answering, following up, reminding, and asking for reviews. Others would rather run everything in one place and move it all over.

Either works. We look at what you're already running and decide on the call, before anyone changes anything.

Will it sound like a robot?

The messages are written for your business and sound like your business. Short, plain, and the way you'd actually say it. You see them before anything goes out, and you can change any of them.

Do I have to learn new software?

No. It runs on your number, and replies come to your phone. If you never open anything new, the follow-up still happens.

Who actually talks to my customers?

The first reply, the nudges, the reminders, and the review requests go out on their own. When someone writes back with a real question, that's you. The same as it is now, just with the customer already warmed up instead of gone.

Is it legal to text customers like this?

Yes, when it's done properly, and doing it properly is part of what you're paying for. Carriers require every business to register before it can send texts, and messages need real consent and a working opt-out. We handle the registration and set the consent up correctly.

Skipping that is why a lot of businesses find their texts quietly stop being delivered. Our own Privacy Policy and Terms show how we handle it.

How soon can it be running?

The build itself is the quick part. The wait is carrier registration. That runs on the carriers' clock, not ours, and it's the one piece nobody can put a firm date on. We start it first so the clock is already running while the rest gets built.

What happens to my leads if I stop?

They're yours. Your contacts and conversation history go with you if you leave.

You already paid for these leads. Let's keep them.

Fifteen minutes. We'll walk your lead path together and find where jobs are falling out, then you decide whether you want it plugged.
