How it works

The path every lead takes.

From the first ring to the check back in months later. Open any stage to see what sets it off, when it fires, and the message that actually goes out.

The stages

  1. What sets it off
    A call comes in, someone submits a web or Facebook form, or you add them by hand.
    When
    Straight away. This is where the path starts.

    No message goes out yet. Nothing sends until we know whether the call was answered.

  2. What sets it off
    The call is answered, or it rings out. Form leads go straight to the instant reply.
    When
    The text goes out within seconds of the missed call.
    The text that goes out

    Sorry we missed your call, this is [Business]. We're out on a job right now. Tell us what you need and we'll get you on the schedule: [booking link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

    Answered calls send nothing. That conversation is yours.

  3. What sets it off
    The lead is tagged as new and still hasn't booked.
    When
    Spread across the following week, by text and email. It stops the moment they book.
    The text that goes out

    Still want us to take a look at that [job type]? Here's the calendar, pick a time that works: [booking link]

  4. What sets it off
    They pick a time on the calendar.
    When
    Confirmation straight away, then reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before the appointment.
    The texts that go out

    You're booked for [day] at [time]. We'll text when we're on the way. Need to change it? [reschedule link]

    [Business] is headed your way, about an hour out. Reply here if anything's changed.

  5. What sets it off
    The appointment on the calendar.
    When
    Nothing sends during the visit.

    No message goes out here. The system waits until the job is marked done.

  6. What sets it off
    The job is marked done.
    When
    The review request goes out the day after. The referral ask follows a few days later.
    The texts that go out

    Thanks for having us out. If we did right by you, a quick review helps more than you'd think: [review link]

    Glad that's sorted. If a neighbour or friend needs the same work, send them our way and we'll take good care of them.

  7. What sets it off
    120 days have passed since the last job.
    When
    A single message, four months out.
    The text that goes out

    It's been a few months since we were out. Anything needing attention before it turns into a bigger job? Here's the calendar: [booking link]

  8. Branch
    What sets it off
    The week of booking nudges ended and they still haven't booked.
    When
    About once a month, ongoing, until they book or opt out.
    The text that goes out

    Heads up, it's about the time of year [seasonal issue] starts showing up. If you want us to take a look, the calendar's here: [booking link]
